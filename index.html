About

How do people come to value, trust and use technologies that are new to them?

I am a marketing scholar and Lecturer at the University of Barishal. I completed my PhD in Management at Huazhong University of Science and Technology (HUST), China, where my thesis examined how the attributes of AI-based digital assistants shape customers’ perceived value and purchase intention in retail. My work combines consumer behavior, digital marketing and innovation management, and it draws on evidence from a least developed country that mainstream literature under-represents.

I am open to postdoctoral opportunities in AI-mediated consumer experience, technology adoption and sustainable consumption.

Research

AI-mediated customer experience. I study how digital assistants’ attributes, including their emotional, immersive and social qualities, create customer value and affect engagement, purchase intention and well-being in AI-driven retail. Related work covers avatar-based marketing and trust in generative AI.

Technology adoption in under-researched contexts. I examine adoption of wearable health devices, telemedicine, IoT smart homes, blockchain-based e-health and ChatGPT among women, older adults, teenagers and students, with a focus on inclusion.

Sustainability and responsible business. My work spans green consumer behavior, green banking, FinTech in sustainable banking, CSR and ESG performance.

Methods. CB-SEM, PLS-SEM, Bayesian SEM, multi-group analysis, fsQCA, in-depth interviews, focus groups and systematic review (TCCM), using SmartPLS, AMOS, SPSS, NVivo and VOSviewer.

Current directions

  • Longitudinal and experimental studies of trust, dependence and well-being in repeated AI assistant use.
  • Cross-national comparisons of AI-enabled service adoption among older adults, women and rural consumers.
  • How AI and FinTech services can support green consumption and responsible finance.
